# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The IPI project welcomes contributors from anywhere in the world. We
pledge to make participation in our project a respectful, harassment-
free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Citing prior work and acknowledging contributions

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual
  attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting / derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (physical address, email
  address, etc.) without explicit permission
- **Disclosing IPI-class vendor findings outside the coordinated
  embargo cycle**
- **Posting private payload / reproducer content in public Issues /
  PRs**
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting

## Project-specific principles

In addition to the general standards above, IPI participants commit
to:

1. **Coordinated disclosure discipline.** Vendor findings respect the
   90-day embargo cycle. No premature public disclosure, even when
   findings are technically interesting.

2. **Methodology honesty.** IPI measures base-model behavioural
   resilience under page-content directive framing — it does not
   validate end-to-end exploitability in named real-world surfaces.
   Contributions should honour this scope distinction in language and
   framing (see `docs/methodology.md`).

3. **Vendor-neutrality in remediation.** Suggested remediation
   patterns in vector taxonomies should be pattern-level
   (vendor-agnostic), not vendor-specific architecture prescriptions.

4. **No vendor-shaming.** The project's purpose is to inform
   consumers and inform vendor pipeline hardening. Public scoreboard
   communications use neutral, professional language.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of
acceptable behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable
behaviour.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban
temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviours that
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: Issues, PRs,
Discussions, project email channels (a private security advisory on this repository),
project social media accounts (when they exist), and any project-
hosted physical or virtual event.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at
a private security advisory on this repository. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security
of reporters of any incident.
